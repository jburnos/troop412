How to Join Troop 412 (Boys)

Open to young men ages 11–17 (or 5th graders with Arrow of Light). Our program combines rank advancement, outdoor skills, and leadership under active youth patrol leaders. Chartered by Olathe Christian Church.

🧭

How to Join Troop 6412 (Girls)

Our thriving linked troop for young women offers the exact same high-adventure, patrol-method Scouting opportunities. We meet simultaneously on Tuesday nights, making family logistics seamless with one drop-off!

📍

Meeting Location & Schedule

Every Tuesday from 7:00 PM – 8:30 PM at Olathe Christian Church. Monthly weekend camping trips year-round across Kansas, Missouri, and premier high-adventure bases.
